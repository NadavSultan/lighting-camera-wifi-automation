## Root-cause evidence and hypothesis

The client emitted `ae({})` for `readSatelliteConfigFromEnv(process.env)`. The helper still accesses `NEXT_PUBLIC_SATELLITE_*` keys, but the object supplied to it was emptied by the production transform. Hypothesis: Vinext replaces direct public `process.env.KEY` references at build time, while passing the whole `process.env` object does not preserve those keys. A post-build client regression failed on the old bundle's missing URL and passed after direct references were added. The new bundle emits all five exact public values together in the call to the provider-neutral helper.
